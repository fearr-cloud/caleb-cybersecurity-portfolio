Reflections & Lessons Learned

Throughout this course and my hands-on labs, I learned that cybersecurity is not just about using tools. It is also about thinking carefully, paying attention to details, and solving problems step by step. Some labs were challenging, but they helped me build confidence and understand how real cybersecurity work is done.

I also learned that communication and documentation matter a lot in IT and cybersecurity. When working on technical issues, it is important to explain what happened, what steps were taken, and how the issue was fixed.

Moving forward, I want to keep improving my knowledge in SOC operations, threat analysis, networking, and incident response while gaining more hands-on experience in real cybersecurity environments.
